About

From mathematics to AI in production

With a Master's in Mathematics (ENS) and a Bachelor's in Computer Science (UPEC), I combine a strong theoretical foundation with several years of hands-on machine learning in real-world settings.

I specialise in LLM engineering and MLOps: from RAG pipelines (chunking, hybrid search, reranking) to multi-agent systems with LangGraph, through fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA) and model security evaluation. My goal: turn complex business problems into AI systems that are robust, observable and scalable.
